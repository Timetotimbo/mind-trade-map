# Changelog

Each version is tagged in git and published as a GitHub release.

## v1.1.0 — 2026-10-06
- **A real mind map.** The atlas is now a map: four columns (market triggers → emotions → behaviors → consequences) with a line for every connection. Tap a card and its whole chain lights up (what causes it and where it leads); tap it again for what to do. Under the map, the chain is spelled out ("Caused by… Leads to… And then…"), handy on a phone where the map scrolls sideways. The card grid is still there under **Cards**.
- **"What are you feeling right now?" check-in.** Tap an emotion (Fear, FOMO, Anger / Revenge…) and see where it leads if nothing changes, the steps to reset, a rule to remember, and a KJV verse. "Show it on the map" lights up its chain. Also reachable from the new "I'm feeling…" button at the top.
- **A fix for every card.** All 38 cards now have practical steps (how to be ready for a trigger, how to reset an emotion, what to do instead of a behavior, how to recover from a consequence), a one-line rule, and a KJV verse.
- In a card's details, the cards it's triggered by and leads to can be tapped to go straight to them.

## v1.0.0 — 2026-09-25
- Atlas of Trading Emotions: the cards, the psychology section and Biblical Insights (KJV), on GitHub Pages.
